Skeptical buyer FAQ

The questions you should be asking

Do we have to replace our current software or buy expensive new platforms?

No. We specialize in Legacy System Bridges. Whether your team runs on spreadsheets, PDFs, QuickBooks, or older desktop software, we build lightweight connectors around the tools you already own.

Is our customer and financial data safe, or does it train public AI models?

Your data stays yours. We run your workflows through commercial API connections whose terms bar using your data to train public AI models. Running and supporting a workflow does mean some data gets stored, such as logs and items waiting in your review queue, so before any work starts we put in writing what is kept, where, and for how long, and we delete it on request.

What happens when the AI encounters a blurry receipt, bad handwriting, or an internet outage?

Real-world data is messy, so we never build blind automation. Our systems include automatic retry logic for temporary outages and route low-confidence items straight to a simple Human Review Queue for your team to approve with one click.

Why is the pilot only $500 when other agencies charge thousands?

Because you should not have to gamble $5,000 on a promise. We scope one focused, high-value bottleneck, build a working solution in days for $500, and let the time savings prove itself before we ever discuss larger projects.

Are we locked into a long-term contract?

Never. You own the workflows and documentation we build for you. Our $99/month Managed Reliability plan is completely optional and month-to-month, covering uptime monitoring, API updates, and direct Michigan-based support.

Do you only work with Michigan businesses?

No. We are proudly based in Durand, Michigan, and we work with businesses everywhere. On-site work is available across Michigan, including Lansing, Flint, Saginaw, Ann Arbor, Grand Rapids and Metro Detroit.
