Paid packages

Our guarantee

First 2 videos within 14 days of an approved script, or your first payment back.
  • Applies to paid packages only and covers the first 2 videos of your first payment.
  • If they aren't delivered within 14 calendar days of script approval (an email reply counts as approval), you get a full refund of your first payment: $150, $300 or $600 (plan fee only: a missed guarantee on Starter with the avatar refunds $150, and on Growth with the avatar refunds $300, because avatar add-on fees are non-refundable once your avatar is set up; on Done-for-You you get the full $600, including the avatar share).
  • You keep whatever was delivered, and service ends with the refund.
  • The clock pauses if we're waiting on assets or feedback from you for more than 3 days, and we'll email you when it pauses and when it resumes.
  • To claim, email railcityai@gmail.com within 14 days of the missed deadline.
  • Refunds go to your original payment method within 7 days of your emailed claim or cancellation, and we cover the processing fees.
  • Filing a chargeback instead of emailing a claim voids the guarantee.
  • Later months have no delivery guarantee.
