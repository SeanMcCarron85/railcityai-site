Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This policy explains how Rail City AI, LLC, based in Durand, Michigan (mailing address: 9169 W State St #4898, Garden City ID 83714) handles your information. Questions: railcityai@gmail.com.

1. Who we serve

Our services are for people 18 and older only. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, business name, email address and phone number when you email railcityai@gmail.com, use the contact form on our website, book a call, or inquire about a service.
  • Intake data: your brand details, goals, audience, topics, scripts, feedback and the assets you send us.
  • Photos, video and voice recordings you provide for a custom avatar.
  • Client business data (automation services): the documents and records you give us access to so we can build and run your workflow, such as invoices, forms, spreadsheets and manuals. These can include information about your own customers and vendors. We handle this data on your behalf and only for your workflow.
  • Payment records: when Stripe is used as our payment processor, we receive payment confirmations and records needed for billing and refunds; we don't store your full card details. Until Stripe checkout is live, payments may be handled by invoice or ACH and we keep the related billing records.

3. Photos, video and voice (biometric-type data)

We treat your face and voice data as sensitive, biometric-type data. We use it only with your signed consent, and only to make your custom avatar and your reels. We never use it for anything else or for anyone else.

4. Processors and AI vendors who process your data

We use Gmail (Google) as our email provider, so emails and files you send us are processed by Google. When you use the contact form on our website, the details you enter (your name, email address, business name, what you're interested in and your message) are sent through Formspree, a form-handling service that delivers them to our email. Formspree may also keep a copy of each message in our Formspree account. Calls are booked through Cal.com, a scheduling service that receives the booking details you enter and sends us the appointment. We use third-party AI tools to generate video, avatars and voice. Your data is shared with them only to produce your reels. Current AI vendors: Runway (image and video generation) and Grok Bot (the AI assistant platform we use to process scripts, briefs and files). Other AI vendors will be listed here once they're in use. When Stripe checkout is live, Stripe processes payments.

For automation services, your workflow sends data to the AI model providers and automation tools named in your written scope. We use only commercial API connections whose terms bar using your data to train public AI models. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security. The site itself sets no advertising or analytics cookies.

5. Retention and deletion

We delete your photos, video, voice data and avatar within 30 days after you withdraw consent or service ends, whichever comes first. Delivered videos are yours to keep; any portfolio copies we keep are covered by the opt-out in our Terms.

For automation services, running and supporting a workflow means some data is stored, such as processing logs and items waiting in your review queue. Your written scope states what is stored, where, and for how long. We delete or return your business data within 30 days after you ask in writing or service ends, whichever comes first, except records we must keep for billing or legal reasons.

6. Sensitive and regulated data

We do not currently sign HIPAA Business Associate Agreements, so our services are not for patient records or other protected health information. Please do not send such records, payment card numbers or government ID numbers through our contact form, booking page or email.

7. No selling of data

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it for advertising.

8. Your choices

To request access to, a copy of, or deletion of your data, or to withdraw avatar consent, email railcityai@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes

We'll post any changes here with a new effective date.
