Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Rail City AI LLC ("Rail City AI", "we") runs this website, railcityai.com. This page explains what information we collect and what we do with it. The short version: very little, and we don't sell it.

What this website collects

  • We don't use analytics, ad trackers, tracking pixels or marketing cookies on this site.
  • We don't have accounts, forms or a newsletter on this site.
  • Our web host, GitHub Pages, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and browser details) for security and to run the service. We don't use those logs to track you.
  • Our headline font loads from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads. See Google's privacy policy for how they handle it.

When you email us

If you email railcityai@gmail.com, we get your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and help you. We don't add you to a mailing list.

Merch orders

Merch is sold and fulfilled by Fourthwall. When you order, Fourthwall collects your name, address, payment details and order information under its own privacy policy. We may see your name, shipping details and order so we can help with problems. We never see your full card number.

Digital downloads

When downloads launch, they'll be sold through a checkout provider that will handle payment and delivery under its own privacy policy. We'll name that provider here before anything goes on sale.

Links to other sites

This site links to YouTube, social media sites and stores. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Some links may become affiliate links in the future, and we'll label them when they do.

Children

This website isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has sent us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing railcityai@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this page, we'll update the date at the top.
