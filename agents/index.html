            How a real build differs

            Same behavior, wired to your business

            The simulators show the rules we build to. Your agent runs on a live AI model with your hours, prices, calendar and documents.

            01 / ANSWERS

            It answers from approved facts

            Hours, prices, service area and policies come from a profile you sign off on. The agent does not make up a price.

            02 / ACTIONS

            It does the next step

            Book the visit, text the confirmation, page the on-call tech, or open a callback task, using the calendar and phone tools you already have.

            03 / HANDOFF

            It hands off when unsure

            Low-confidence questions go to a person with the full transcript. You choose the threshold and who gets the handoff.

            Callers and chat visitors are told they are talking to an AI assistant. We do not build agents for patient records or other regulated data today.
