Video questions

What owners ask before they start

Will people know the videos are AI?

Yes, and that is on purpose. Every reel carries a visible AI label on screen and in the caption, and we turn on each platform's AI-content setting. Our presenters never pose as real employees or customers, and we do not make fake testimonials or reviews.

What does the free video pilot cost me?

Nothing. You get 2 free reels in exchange for a required honest testimonial, and it does not have to be positive. There are 5 spots. Pilot reels use our AI spokesperson or AI voiceover, and any testimonial we publish notes that you received free reels.

What if I do not like the script?

Nothing is produced until you approve the script. We draft it, you review it, and production starts only after you say yes.

What if you miss the deadline?

On paid packages, your first 2 videos arrive within 14 days of an approved script or you get your first payment back. The full terms are in the guarantee section above.

Who owns the reels?

You do, once the package is paid in full. We may keep copies for our portfolio unless you opt out in writing.

Can the presenter be me instead of Maya?

Yes. A custom avatar of your own likeness is a $100 add-on, included in the Done-for-You plan, and is made only with your signed consent to use your face and voice. It is not available in the free pilot.

How long is a reel and where can I post it?

A reel is one finished vertical video, 15 to 60 seconds long, delivered ready to post on the platforms you already use, such as Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Starter is a one-time purchase. Monthly plans renew each month until you cancel by email, and the cancellation and refund rules are listed under the plans above.
