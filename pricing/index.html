Payment and refunds

How paying us works

Plain rules, stated before you spend anything.

01 / SCOPE

A written scope comes first

After your free X-Ray you get a written scope: what we will build, the price, the start date, any third-party software or usage costs, and what data is handled. You pay nothing before you have it.

02 / PAYMENT

Half up front, half on delivery

For builds, 50% is due as a deposit before the build starts. The other 50% is due when we deliver the working build and walk your team through it.

03 / REFUNDS

Your deposit is refundable until the build starts

Change your mind before the start date and you get the whole deposit back. After the build starts, we keep 25% of the deposit plus a share of the rest that matches the work completed, and refund what is left.

Example: $500 Pilot Build, $250 deposit
Cancel before the build starts: $250.00 back
Cancel as the build starts: $187.50 back
Cancel when it is half built: $93.75 back

04 / MONTHLY

Monthly plans are month-to-month

Monitoring and agent plans have no minimum term. Cancel any time by email to stop the next charge. What we built stays yours and keeps running as built.

Video packages are paid up front and carry their own delivery guarantee and refund rules. See the video plans and our Terms.

Pricing questions

What people ask before they book

What is the difference between the $250 Starter and the $500 Pilot Build?

The Starter automates one simple, well-defined task, such as sending a follow-up text after a missed call. The Pilot Build covers one real bottleneck that needs more care: document extraction, automatic retries when a service is down, and a human review queue for anything the system is not sure about.

What do the monthly fees cover?

Hosting, monitoring and updates for the agent or assistant we built, plus direct support. Any third-party software or usage costs are listed in your written scope before you pay, so there are no surprise charges.

Can a small business afford this?

That is the point of the Local business column. The X-Ray is free, builds start at $250, half is due up front, and nothing locks you in. If automation is not worth it for your situation, we will tell you on the call.

How do large-organization quotes work?

We scope the work on a call, then send a fixed quote in writing with phases, so you can start with a pilot group and expand. Security questionnaires and written data-handling terms are part of that process.

When will training be available?

We are building the training program now and will post pricing when it opens. Send a message and choose "Training (notify me)" to hear first.
